I'm Amarthya — Glaks to most people — an AI/ML engineer who graduated in 2026 with a B.E. in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science from Bearys Institute of Technology, Mangalore.

My work sits at the intersection of applied AI and real infrastructure: agent orchestration, retrieval pipelines, and the serverless plumbing that keeps them running in production.
